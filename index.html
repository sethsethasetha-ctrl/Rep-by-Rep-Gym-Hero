Your route to Gym Hero

  1. Choose an unlocked challenge and review its target muscle group.
  2. Plan workouts and recovery across a limited number of training days.
  3. Reach the power target while controlling fatigue and avoiding injury.
  4. Earn stars and rewards, upgrade your gear, and take on the next challenge.

The game includes 12 progressive challenges, local progress saving, original music, and optional rewarded advertising. No account is required for the core game.
