Rep by Rep: Gym Hero

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

Overview

This policy explains how Rep by Rep: Gym Hero handles information. The core game does not require an account. Gameplay remains available if advertising configuration cannot be loaded or an advertisement is unavailable.

Information stored on your device

The game stores progress and preferences locally using Apple platform storage. This may include unlocked challenges, stars, coins, equipment level, muscle statistics, completed challenges, and the music setting. You can remove this information with the in-game reset option or by deleting the app.

Advertising and tracking permission

The game uses Yandex Mobile Ads to offer optional rewarded advertisements. It requests permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before accessing the advertising identifier. If you deny permission, the app does not read the IDFA, although Yandex may still provide contextual or non-personalized advertising where available.

Yandex and its included AppMetrica components may process advertising identifiers when permitted, device and network information, IP address, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic information under their own policies. Rewarded ads are optional and are not required to use the core game.

Security and service configuration

On physical iOS devices, the app initializes the HSSecurity SDK for security and abuse-prevention purposes. The integration supplies the app's bundle identifier and an identifier derived from Apple's Identifier for Vendor, and the SDK may process device or risk signals needed to protect the service.

The app also contacts a configuration service to retrieve the active Yandex rewarded-ad placement. The request includes the app's bundle identifier. The game does not use this request to create a user account.

Sharing and third parties

Information needed for advertising, measurement, diagnostics, configuration, or security may be processed by Yandex, its AppMetrica components, HSSecurity, and the configuration service provider. Their processing is governed by their respective terms and privacy practices. You can review the Yandex Privacy Policy.

Data retention and choices

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you reset progress or delete the app. Third-party providers retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations. You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings and can choose not to watch rewarded ads.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game's features, SDKs, or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
